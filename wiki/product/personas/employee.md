## Summary

The employee works at a company that a snack shop vendor serves, and takes snacks from the vendor's shop. They use a phone at the shop, have one personal sign-in on one phone at a time, and record only their own purchases.


## Goals

- Record their everyday purchase quickly and easily, on their own phone.
- Record their own purchases themselves, without one employee having to record everyone's.


## Pain points

- One employee in the company has to record a whole day's purchases from everyone and give the bill to the manager.


## Behaviors

- Takes snacks at the shop and records them on a phone there.
