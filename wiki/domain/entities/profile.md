## Definition

A profile is the details that identify an employee in Sundal: their picture, their name and the mobile number they sign in with. It belongs to one employee. The employee can see it but cannot change it. The company admin can fill in its details.

## Attributes

- image — image — a picture of the employee.
- name — text — the employee's name.
- mobile number — phone number — what the employee signs in with.

## Invariants

None.

## Lifecycle

A profile exists once the employee is added to Sundal.

## Related entities

None.
