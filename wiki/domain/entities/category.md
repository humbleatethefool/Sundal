## Definition

A category is a named group of items in a shop's catalog, such as drinks or chips. It is not an item.

## Attributes

- name — text — what the category is called.
- image — image — a picture of the category.

## Invariants

None.

## Lifecycle

A category exists once the shop names it. It then holds the items the shop adds to it.
