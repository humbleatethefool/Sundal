## Summary

A company admin is a person at a company who handles its bills and pays the shops and suppliers its staff buy from. They usually meet a shop through a bill, not through the day's purchases, and settle what the company owes on a [[concepts/running-account]] once a month. The [[personas/office-employee]] and the [[personas/snack-shop-vendor]] are the people whose purchases make up that bill.

## Goals

- Pay the right amount to each shop once a month.
- Know the total the company owes without collecting the details of every purchase.
- Pay on time, so the shop keeps serving the company's staff.

## Pain points

- A bill put together by one colleague from memory can be wrong or late.
- A bill that nobody can check leads to disputes with the shop.
- Collecting totals from several colleagues and several shops adds work at month end.

## Behaviors

- Handles bills in a batch once a month rather than one at a time.
- Checks a bill's total against what the company usually owes before paying.
- Relies on colleagues to pass a bill along, because the admin is not at the shop.

## Quotes

None.

## Anti-goals

- Disputes about a bill that nobody can check.
- Extra steps to find out a monthly total.
