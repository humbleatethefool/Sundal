## Summary

A snack shop vendor runs a small shop that sells snacks and drinks, often alone and mostly to regular customers. They serve people face to face at a counter or stall, and often keep accounts for regular customers who pay later.

## Goals

- Keep the items customers ask for in stock.
- Know what each regular customer has taken.
- Be paid in full for what was taken, on time.

## Pain points

- The shop is busy at peak times, so a purchase on credit can go unwritten.
- The stock count in a notebook or in memory drifts from what is on the shelf.
- Chasing late payment of a balance on a [[concepts/running-account]].

## Behaviors

- Serves customers in person, standing at the counter or stall.
- Keeps accounts for regular customers in a notebook or from memory.
- Restocks from a wholesaler or market when [[concepts/stock]] runs low, often judging it by eye.

## Quotes

None.

## Anti-goals

- Anything that slows serving a customer at the counter.
- Extra work during the busy hours.
