## Summary

An office employee works at a company and buys snacks and drinks from a shop near their workplace during the day. They usually take a short break, buy from the same shop most days, and sometimes buy on a [[concepts/running-account]] that the company settles with the [[personas/snack-shop-vendor]].

## Goals

- Get a snack or a drink quickly, without a long break from work.
- Buy without carrying cash.
- Know what they have taken, so a bill at the end of the month holds no surprise.

## Pain points

- A busy counter at break time means waiting in a queue.
- A purchase taken without paying depends on someone else writing it down correctly.
- A bill for the month is hard to check when nobody kept a record of each purchase.

## Behaviors

- Takes breaks at the same times each day and buys from the same nearby shop.
- Buys a few items at a time, usually the same ones.
- Checks a bill only when the total looks wrong.

## Quotes

None.

## Anti-goals

- A purchase process longer than the break it happens in.
- Being charged for something they did not take.
