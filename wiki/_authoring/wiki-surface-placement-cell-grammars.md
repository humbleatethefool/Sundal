# Wiki Surface Placement Cell Grammars

The bullets of a `ui-surface` page that say how a surface is shown, where it sits, whom it serves and which patterns it follows. `wiki-cell-grammars` holds how a template declares a grammar, and the rules every grammar shares.

**See also:** `wiki-authoring-spec` — for the readability rules and the list of every author-facing lookup.
**See also:** `wiki-cell-grammars` — for the `Cells:` label, pending cells and what stays judgment.
**See also:** `wiki-surface-cell-grammars` — for the Actions and Flow steps grammars and how a platform copy of a bullet reads.
**See also:** `wiki-sitemap-cell-grammars` — for the grammar the Node bullet takes.
**See also:** `wiki-journey-cell-grammars` — for the grammar the Journey stages bullet takes.
**See also:** `wiki-layout-pattern-cell-grammars` — for the Regions table whose Ids the Opened from bullet names.

## Surface placement cell grammars

- **Platforms** — the platforms the surface is shown on, a `platform-list` value: `,`-separated
  values from the page's `platforms:`, with no repeats and no `None.`. The bullet is optional and
  takes no platform copy. A value the page lacks is dropped, and when the bullet is absent, does not
  parse or names no platform the page lists, the surface takes the page's platforms. The audit
  errors on a value outside the page's `platforms:` (`platform-list/not-on-page`), a repeated value
  (`platform-list/repeated`) and `None.` (`platform-list/none`). Every check that reads the
  surface's platforms reads these, so a surface that lists no screen platform takes `None.` for
  Container and Node.
- **Container** — how the surface is shown on a platform: `page`, `dialog` or `panel`, a `one-of`
  value. `None.` when the surface lists no screen platform (`web` or `mobile`); on a screen platform
  the Container that applies is never `None.`.
- **Node** — the sitemap nodes the surface sits on, `node-refs`: one
  `[[design:information-architecture/<page>]] <node-id>` per application the surface is placed in,
  separated by `;`, or `None.`. Which platforms need one, and which take `None.`, is
  `wiki-sitemap-cell-grammars` §How a surface cites a node; `surface-placement` checks it, an error.
  `surface-unreached` is a warning, and fires only on a platform some application page lists: it
  reports a listed node that no application's composed sitemap includes on that platform, and a
  surface with a screen platform but no node there that no application reaches. A surface with no
  node is placed in an application on a platform when a surface placed in that application leads to
  it or shows it on that platform, or its Opened from places it there (the Opened from bullet
  below), and the chain through other surfaces with no node counts. A surface placed in no application, such as a summons with no
  screen, starts no chain. The warning reads "it sits on no node there, no surface placed in an
  application leads to it or shows it, and no region of a pattern the application cites opens it";
  to clear it, let a placed surface lead to it, name the surface it is shown in or the pattern
  region it is opened from, or name the node it sits on in that application.
- **Shown in** — the surfaces a region, a named surface shown inside another surface's page, is
  shown within, `surface-refs`:
  `[[design:surfaces/<module>/<feature-slug>#<surface-anchor>]]` links separated by `;`, each
  with the `design:` prefix and the anchor of a named surface, or `None.`. An entry may end with a
  scope (`wiki-surface-cell-grammars` § Surface cell grammars), and then counts only where the
  scope holds; one surface named in two entries with different scopes counts in the applications
  of either. The bullet is optional. A platform copy (`**Shown in (web)**`) overrides the shared
  value for its platform. A region with Node `None.` is placed in every application where an
  entry counts and the surface it names is placed, through that surface's Shown in and Leads to
  chains too, and sits on that surface's nodes. Surfaces shown in one another with none placed
  leave all of them unplaced. The audit errors on an entry that is not a
  link (`surface-refs/unparseable`), `None.` mixed with a link (`surface-refs/none-mixed`), a link
  to a component (`surface-refs/wrong-target`), a link without the `design:` prefix
  (`surface-refs/wrong-prefix`) or without an anchor (`surface-refs/missing-anchor`), a link to a
  page that is not a `ui-surface` (`surface-refs/wrong-type`), an anchor naming a heading that is
  not a named surface (`surface-refs/not-a-named-surface`) and a bad scope
  (`surface-refs/wrong-scope`). `surface-placement` also errors
  on a Shown in naming a surface on a platform where the applied Node names a node, since a
  surface sits on a node or in a surface it is shown in and never both, and on a Shown in on a
  surface that lists no screen platform.
- **Opened from** — the layout-pattern regions a dialog or panel opens from when it opens from the
  page's chrome, such as an account menu in the shell's sidebar, rather than from a surface,
  `pattern-region-refs`: `[[design:layout-patterns/<slug>]] <region-id>` entries separated by `;`,
  each with an optional scope (`wiki-surface-cell-grammars` § Surface cell grammars), or `None.`.
  The region Id is an Id of the pattern's Regions table (`wiki-layout-pattern-cell-grammars`). The
  bullet is optional. A platform copy (`**Opened from (web)**`) overrides the shared value for its
  platform. An entry counts in an application on a platform when a page of the application's
  composed sitemap cites the pattern in `layout-patterns:`, the pattern's own `platforms` include
  the platform, and the entry's scope holds. A surface with Node `None.` on the platform is then
  placed there. It sits on no node, since it opens over whatever page is showing, and leads on
  through its own Leads to as any placed surface does. A pattern nested inside a page, such as a
  detail layout, places the surface in every application citing it, even where only some of the
  application's pages carry the opener. A dialog opened from a page's content takes a Leads to
  from the surfaces on that page instead. The audit errors on an entry in no known form
  (`pattern-region-refs/unparseable`), `None.` mixed with an entry
  (`pattern-region-refs/none-mixed`), a link without the `design:` prefix
  (`pattern-region-refs/wrong-prefix`), a link to a page that is not a `layout-pattern`
  (`pattern-region-refs/wrong-type`), a region Id the pattern's Regions table lacks
  (`pattern-region-refs/unknown-region`) and a bad scope (`pattern-region-refs/wrong-scope`).
  Whether the named region really opens the surface stays the author's judgment.
  `surface-placement` also errors on an Opened from naming a region on a platform where the
  applied Node names a node or the applied Shown in names a surface, and on an Opened from on a
  surface that lists no screen platform.
- **Journey stages** — the journey stages and opportunities the surface serves, a `journey-stages`
  value (`wiki-journey-cell-grammars`). Any container or kind may serve a stage.
- **Layout patterns** — `pattern-links`: `None.`, or `[[design:layout-patterns/<slug>]]` links
  separated by `,`. The audit errors on an entry that is not a link and on a link to a page that is
  not a `layout-pattern`. `layout-pattern-fit` warns when a cited pattern's `platforms` lacks the
  platform the citation applies on, or its `kinds` lacks the surface's kind. `layout-pattern-coverage`
  warns on a `layout-pattern` page that no `information-architecture` page cites in `layout-patterns:`,
  crediting a pattern that a page the application includes cites, and says whether a surface cites it.
- **Heading kind** — the `(<kind>)` in a surface's heading, one of the surface kinds. A kind says what
  the surface is for and not how it is shown, so `panel` is a Container and no kind. The kinds, each
  with a one-line definition, are the `kinds` of `.agent-kit/dist/wiki/scripts/data/design-vocab.json`.
  `surface-kind` errors on a heading with no kind or one outside them, on a page stamped at or above
  the template version that added Container.
